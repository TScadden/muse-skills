# Faith Post Design From the Established Formats

## Start from the established format

- Identify the post category before designing anything, and load that category format first. White and cream means Hebrew posts, black means Dave Butler type devotional posts, and deep navy with parchment text and gold accents means deep study posts.

- Open a finished post from the same category, or a vault entry for that category, and copy its layout system before you write copy. Match its background treatment, title placement, type system, spacing, and slide rhythm.

- Build deep study posts as visual led plates with one or two short lines per slide, crisp text over the illustration, gold italic capital titles set left, a gold rule, and dot bullets with key words in gold.

- Research a general design standard only when no established format covers the request. A patched screenshot collage with mixed type styles and black bars is a reject, never a delivery option.

## Verify the content before the design

- Confirm every factual claim with three independent sources before shipping, and put the sources on a final sources slide for deep study posts.

- Keep each person or team in a multi subject post distinct. Verify what each one actually said or did from its own source, and never claim two subjects run the same way or teach the same method unless a source says so directly.

- Present scholarship as a reading of the scholarship, never as direct doctrine, and draw deep study claims only from canon scripture and peer reviewed sources the user has approved for this work.

- Write slide copy in the user voice with names capitalized, keep captions in normal capitalization, and keep post copy free of dashes of every kind by rewriting with commas, colons, or periods.

## Present and deliver the set

- Cut any option your own quality check flags as bad before delivery, and present only designs you would defend.

- Deliver the finished set grouped and sorted by post, with a unique filename on every image and every revised file versioned.

- Build a TikTok version at 9 by 16 with exactly five broad hashtags in the caption when the user asks for one, and keep slide text centered unless the established format for that category sets titles left.

- TikTok safe zone (measured from a real posted carousel): TikTok's photo player and feed UI cover roughly the top 200px (the Following/For You bar) and everything below about y1640 of 1920 (the caption panel). Keep every important element inside that band: the swipe pill's bottom edge at least 280px from the bottom, top anchored final slides starting at y 210 or lower, and the sources footer bar ending by about y1630 with the follow pill above it.
